## How Inflation Impacts Real Estate Investment

Inflation reduces the purchasing power of cash, meaning that money held in the bank gradually loses value. Real estate, in contrast, often acts as a hedge against inflation due to several factors:

* Rising construction costs: Inflation increases the cost of building materials and labor, which in turn raises property replacement costs.
* Limited land supply: Prime plots and apartments in well-developed areas like Bahria Town remain scarce, preserving their value.
* Rental growth potential: Rental rates often increase with inflation, offering steady income for investors.
* Long-term appreciation: Properties in reputable developments tend to appreciate faster during periods of high inflation.

However, not all properties are immune. Poorly located apartments, projects with delayed possession, or units by inexperienced [property dealers in Karachi](https://www.narkinsbuilders.com/) may not deliver the same returns. Therefore, careful evaluation is key.
